## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon judges watching a 3-minute demo and lighting/show designers (think facade-show studios). Context: a laptop screen recording at 1080p and live finals. The job: upload a building photo, see the architecture read, pick a lighting concept, generate and check the show.

### Brand Personality
Precise, cinematic, unshowy. Control-room confidence: the lit building is the hero, the interface steps back.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme (users work with night imagery and bright LED footage; chrome must not glare). Palette drawn from the building and its light: deep graphite neutrals tinted slightly warm, with a champagne/amber LED accent used sparingly. No purple or blue gradients, no neon-on-dark, no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no side-stripe borders. Distinctive display face plus a refined body face (not the reflex-font list). Desktop only; type and controls must read at 1080p in a screen recording. Respect reduced motion.

### Design Principles
1. The building leads: imagery and fixtures get the space, controls stay quiet.
2. Every AI step is labelled with the Meta model that ran it (SAM 3.1, Muse Spark 1.3, Muse Image 1.0); this is a submission requirement and stays visible.
3. Show state honestly: progress, fallbacks (e.g. procedural texture) and failures are stated plainly.
4. One accent, used rarely; hierarchy comes from size, weight and space, not decoration.
5. Short, concrete copy; no marketing filler.
