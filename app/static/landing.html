How it works

Five steps from photo to checked show. Each AI step names the model that ran it. The motion is our own code.

  1. 01

    Photo

    Upload one view of the building, or four sides to stitch into a 360 degree facade map.

    Your photo
  2. 02

    Element map

    Fins, glass bands, setbacks and the crown, outlined as masks. Muse Spark proposes the boxes and SAM 3.1 traces them.

    SAM 3.1Muse Spark 1.3
  3. 03

    Show plan

    Lighting concepts, then a timed plan whose steps point at real element ids. A plan that fails that check is re-asked once.

    Muse Spark 1.3
  4. 04

    Motion

    Muse Image makes the light-art texture for each concept; if that call fails we use a procedural texture and say so. Our code then moves it along the element outlines.

    Muse Image 1.0Our own code
  5. 05

    Readability check

    A fixture-pitch audit plus a vision review of a rendered still, with an automatic fix for what it flags.

    Muse Spark 1.3

Measured on two photos: mapping took 54 and 59 seconds, and map, plan and check together take about two and a half minutes. Mask accuracy is not measured against ground truth. Details in METRICS.md.
